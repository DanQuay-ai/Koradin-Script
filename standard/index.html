1. Scope

Kɔradin is a featural syllabary for the Akan languages (Twi, Fante, Akuapem, Bono and related varieties), created by Ntirikwaamaa in 2021. This Standard fixes the sign repertoire and the writing rules of version 1.0. Later versions will add a Kɔradin orthography of its own (section 8).

2. Structure of a sign

  • A sign writes one syllable: an onset radical joined to a vowel form.
  • Seven vowels: a, e, ɛ, i, o, ɔ, u. A vowel alone has its own sign.
  • One onset family shares one radical. Families: h w y p b kp gb t ky tw d j gy dw k g kw s z hy f v hw r l n m ny my (29).
  • Labiovelars (kp, gb) and Akan clusters (ky, gy, hy, tw, dw, kw, hw, ny, my) are families of their own, not two signs.
  • The repertoire is 205 syllable signs plus 7 vowel signs (chart, section 9). Gaps in the chart are syllables Akan does not use; they stay empty.

3. Nasals

  • Opening nasal (a syllabic n, m or ng before a syllable): a mark written before the sign. nkwa → n-kwa.
  • Closing nasal (n, m, ng ending a syllable): a mark centred under the sign. akan → a-ka(n), kɔm → kɔ(m).
  • A doubled m before a consonant is written once, as a closing mark: yɛmmpɛ → yɛm-pɛ. Before a vowel, the opening mark stays: mma → m-ma, nnipa → n-ni-pa.

4. Consonants without a vowel

  • Clusters (CRV): a consonant before r or l takes a filler vowel: ɔ before a back vowel (a, o, ɔ, u), ɛ before a front vowel (e, ɛ, i). fra → fɔ-ra, tre → tɛ-re.
  • Codas: a consonant closing a syllable before another sign takes the same filler: dawurbɔ → da-wu-rɔ-bɔ.
  • Final consonant: takes ɛ. tsitsir → ti-ti-rɛ, bayir → ba-yi-rɛ.
  • Lone w: written with the ɔ sign. asɛmpɔw → a-sɛm-pɔ-ɔ.
  • Some words take the echo vowel instead of ɔ/ɛ (mbra → m-ba-ra). These are fixed word by word in the Kɔradin word list.

5. Reduplication

  • A repeated syllable is written once, with a dash above it: ntomtom → n-tom with a dash over tom, ahwɛhwɛ → a-hwɛ with a dash over hwɛ.

5b. Fante

  • ts and dz are written with the t and d families: tse → te, dzidzi → di-di, mfantse → m-fa(n)-te.
  • Elided forms are written in full: n'asɛm → ne asɛm (see Abakah 2004, Elision in Fante).

6. Words, spaces, punctuation

  • Signs within a word sit close together; words are separated by a wide space.
  • Punctuation: Kɔradin has three marks only: comma, exclamation mark and question mark.
  • Direction: left to right, lines top to bottom.

7. Sort order

By onset family in the order of section 2, then by vowel in the order a e ɛ i o ɔ u; vowel-only signs first; an opening nasal sorts before its syllable, a closing nasal after.

8. Planned (version 2)

  • A Kɔradin orthography by sound, independent of Latin spelling (asɛmpɔw → a-sɛm-pɔ-o).
  • Latin homographs may be written differently in Kɔradin.
